BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE

A route to
your voice.

UNMUTE is designed for eighth-grade adolescent EFL learners in a rural context, with an estimated A1–A2 level. It responds to a familiar challenge: learners understand basic English structures but hesitate to speak.

Different support. Shared purpose.

The story connects listening and noticing, language organisation, communication strategies and question practice. Support gradually reduces as learners move towards short oral exchanges. The cast represents available support, not fixed learner types.

Learning objective

Across the full resource, learners will ask and answer at least four daily-routine questions using Yes/No questions with do / does and WH-questions with what, what time and where, with pronunciation and clarity sufficient for a partner to understand.

This experience

Meet the four friends, explore your speaking mission and begin with Kai's Morning Meltdown. The teacher facilitates, models and supports interaction.

Keyboard and touch navigation · Optional reduced motion · No microphone or account needed for this introduction.
